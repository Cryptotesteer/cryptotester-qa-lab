# CryptoTester QA Lab 🧪

Web3 product testing, QA research, exploratory testing and documented
product findings by CryptoTester.

## About

I test Web3 applications, dApps and digital products to identify
functional issues, user-experience problems, edge cases and
transaction-related risks.

My testing focuses on understanding how a product behaves not only
when everything goes right, but also when users make mistakes,
transactions fail, inputs are invalid, or unexpected conditions occur.

## Testing Philosophy

A good QA report should answer:

- What was tested?
- Why was it tested?
- What was expected?
- What actually happened?
- Can the result be reproduced?
- What evidence supports the finding?
- How serious is the impact?
- Is it a confirmed defect or an observation?
- What should be tested after the fix?

I do not invent defects to make a report look more impressive.

When the intended behaviour is unclear, I document the behaviour as an
observation or product question until there is enough evidence to
classify it as a defect.
